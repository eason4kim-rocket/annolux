# Annolux launch workspace

This directory contains public-safe launch preparation. It never authorizes an
external post, bulk message, paid promotion, or account change.

## Release sequence

1. Recruit 10–20 real Agent/RAG developers for a private free beta.
2. Require at least 10 people to complete a first Search call and at least 3 to
   use the API repeatedly before describing the beta as validated.
3. Publish the repository, GHCR images, OpenAPI, Benchmarks, and Lab Notes.
4. Launch the free product and Playground. Paddle Live is not a dependency.
5. The user personally posts Show HN, then Product Hunt after free stability,
   followed by relevant Reddit, V2EX, Juejin, and Zhihu communities.

The fact pack is the source for copy. Never add a testimonial, company logo,
user count, latency number, uptime statement, or benchmark result that has not
been independently verified and dated.

## Files

- `fact-pack.md`: locked product facts and claims that are safe to make.
- `beta-program.md`: invite, onboarding, observation, and exit criteria.
- `launch-copy.md`: drafts for user-operated public launches.
- `assets-checklist.md`: screenshots, link inventory, and UTM convention.
- `support-playbook.md`: private support and incident-response templates.
- `metrics.md`: privacy-preserving launch metrics and decision gates.
- `gefei-seo-playbook.md`: research notes on 哥飞's public SEO method.
- `gefei-seo-annolux.md`: how that method maps onto Annolux (advice only).
